wrapped templates

the stats are yours, but the look is up to you. templates change the whole design of your poster: background, colors, where the lists sit, how big the album art is. grab one from the library, or design your own in the visual editor. no code, no account, no strings.

how it works

the template library has official themes and templates artists have made and submitted. search, filter by category, sort by featured, newest or most used, then hit use this template. it drops you straight into the generator with that look applied.

or open the template editor if you'd rather make your own: a live Wrapped-sized canvas where you drag, resize and style text, ranked lists, big-number stats, backgrounds and album art. when it looks good, submit it for review and it can go live in the library.

a template is just data, so it works with every service the generator supports: listenbrainz, last.fm, libre.fm, and self-hosted navidrome.

what a template controls

background - a solid color, a gradient, or an image you upload.

palette - the label and value colors for headings and the big numbers.

elements - text, ranked lists (top artists, top tracks), big-number stats (minutes listened, top genre), each with its own font, weight, size, color and position.

album art - where the record sits, how big it is, and whether it has a frame.

faq

does swapping a template change my stats? no. your artists, tracks, minutes and genre stay the same; only the design changes.

do i need an account to browse or use templates? no. that's all open. only submitting one ties a creator identity to your browser.

how do my templates get into the library? submit one in the editor, it waits for a maintainer to review it, then it appears publicly under your creator handle.

is it free? yes, and open source (agpl). code's on github if you want to self-host.
