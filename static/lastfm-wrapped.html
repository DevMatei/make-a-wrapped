last.fm wrapped

you've been scrobbling for years and all you get is a yearly playback page. this turns your last.fm history into an actual spotify wrapped style poster: top artists, top tracks, top genre, and estimated listening minutes. any month, any year, whenever you feel like it.

make your last.fm wrapped

how it works

type your last.fm username, pick a time period, hit generate. that's it. your scrobbles are public on last.fm so there's no login and no password, we just read the same data anyone can see on your profile.

artist artwork gets pulled automatically from last.fm (with musicbrainz and wikidata as backup), or you can upload and position your own image. a few themes to pick from, then save the poster and post it wherever.

faq

do i need my last.fm password? no. just the username.

can i pick a random month from two years ago? yes. custom date ranges are the whole point.

how are the minutes calculated? last.fm doesn't give total listening time, so it's estimated from your scrobble count times the real lengths of your most played tracks. close enough to brag with.

is it free? yes, and open source (agpl). code's on github if you want to self-host.

not on last.fm?

the same generator works with listenbrainz and self-hosted navidrome too. same poster, different source.
