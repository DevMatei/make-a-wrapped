make a wrapped / spotify wrapped alternative

a spotify wrapped alternative

spotify wrapped is fun, but only if you use spotify, and only once a year. make a wrapped gives you the same kind of poster (top artists, top tracks, top genre, minutes listened) from wherever your listening history actually lives. listenbrainz, last.fm, libre.fm, your own navidrome or maloja server, or just numbers you type in. any month, any year, free, no login.

where it gets your stats from

  • listenbrainz: public, open, just your username.
  • last.fm and libre.fm: your scrobbles, just your username.
  • navidrome: runs in your browser, so your server password never leaves your device.
  • sonosphere: just your username, same as listenbrainz.
  • maloja and koito: point it at your self hosted server and it reads the public charts.
  • rocksky: your bluesky handle.
  • custom: no service at all? type your own top 5 artists, tracks, minutes and genre and get the poster anyway.

apple music, youtube music, tidal, deezer...

most players can scrobble to last.fm or listenbrainz, either built in or through an app like pano scrobbler or web scrobbler. turn that on, give it a while to collect plays, then come back and make your wrapped. if you just want a poster right now, the custom source works with whatever you remember listening to.

how it compares to spotify wrapped

honest version. spotify wrapped has fancier animations and spotify's own data. this one works any day instead of only in december, does any month or year you pick, has templates and a visual editor, readme badges, and is open source under agpl so you can self host it. if you are on spotify you can use both, nothing stops you.

faq

do i need a spotify account? no. it never talks to spotify.

can i make one before december? yes. this year so far, last year, a single month, or all time.

how are minutes worked out? listenbrainz and the scrobble services don't store total listening time, so it's estimated from your play count and the real lengths of your most played tracks. on custom you type the number yourself.

is it free? yes, no account, and the code is on github.
