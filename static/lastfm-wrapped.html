last.fm wrapped

this is the last.fm wrapped generator from make a wrapped, a free open source tool for turning scrobbles into a spotify wrapped style poster. you have been scrobbling for years and all you get is a yearly playback page. this turns your last.fm history into top artists, top tracks, top genre, and estimated listening minutes. make your 2025 wrapped now, or track 2026 so far, any month, any year, whenever you feel like it. just your username, no login.

how it works

  1. type your last.fm username. scrobbles are public so there is no login and no password.
  2. pick a time period: 2025, 2026 so far, last month, or any custom month or year.
  3. hit generate, then save the poster and post it wherever.

artist artwork gets pulled automatically from last.fm (with musicbrainz and wikidata as backup), or you can upload and position your own image. a few themes to pick from.

why use this one

honest version, no hype. the official last.fm playback is december only and pretty limited. this one runs any day, does any month or year, needs no login, is free and open source under agpl, and lets you self host. that combo is why people keep using make a wrapped for their yearly recap.

faq

do i need my last.fm password? no. just the username.

can i pick a random month from two years ago? yes. custom date ranges are the whole point.

how are the minutes calculated? last.fm does not give total listening time, so it is estimated from your scrobble count times the real lengths of your most played tracks. close enough to brag with.

does last.fm have an official wrapped? last.fm has a yearly playback page each december. this gives you the same idea on demand for 2025, 2026, or any month.

when does last.fm wrapped come out? the official one lands in december. you can generate yours here right now without waiting.

is it free? yes, and open source (agpl). code is on github if you want to self-host.

not on last.fm?

the same generator works with make a wrapped home, listenbrainz and self-hosted navidrome too. same poster, different source.
