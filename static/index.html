why this exists

everyone posts their spotify wrapped every year and if you share a listenbrainz or last.fm one people go "wait what's that?". this bridges that gap. i swapped spotify for navidrome but still wanted that wrapped moment. now you can have it too.

no login for public stats, navidrome stays fully in your browser, and you're not stuck waiting for december. more about the last.fm wrapped, the navidrome wrapped and templates.

it's foss

agpl licensed and officially listed on the listenbrainz enabled applications page. star the repo, open issues, or contribute if you want. or just use it, that's cool too. there's also a discord for bug reports, template ideas and arguing about music.
