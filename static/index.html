your friends post spotify wrapped every year and you're sitting there with your listenbrainz/last.fm/navidrome stats like "i have taste too". now you can prove it, whenever you want, not just in december. drop your username, pick a service and a time frame (this year, last month, a random month from 2 years ago, whatever), and get a poster with your top artists, tracks, listening time, and genre.

pick a finished month. future months are hidden.

connect to your navidrome server directly. everything runs in your browser, credentials never leave this page.

use your normal login or create an app-specific token. requests go through the subsonic api to your server.

why this exists

everyone posts their spotify wrapped every year and if you share a listenbrainz or last.fm one people go "wait what's that?". this bridges that gap. i swapped spotify for navidrome but still wanted that wrapped moment. now you can have it too.

you're not stuck waiting for december either, generate one for any month, last year, whatever period you want. share wrapped.devmatei.com with your friends if you want.

on last.fm or self-hosting? there's more about the last.fm wrapped and the navidrome wrapped specifically.
