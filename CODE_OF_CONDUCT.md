# code of conduct

short version: be decent to each other. this is a hobby project, keep it chill.

## the pledge

we want make a wrapped to be a welcoming space for anyone who wants to pitch in. harassment and bigotry of any kind are not ok, full stop. we're all here because we like music, data, and self-hosting, not to make anyone feel bad.

## what counts as not ok

- personal attacks, insults, trolling, or being a jerk in issues, prs, or reviews.
- hate speech, slurs, or discrimination based on race, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, or religion.
- doxxing or sharing anyone's private info without permission.
- harassment in dms or other platforms because of this project.
- spamming, flame wars, or generally being hostile.

## how to act

- be patient with people who are still learning.
- assume good faith, ask for clarification before assuming something was done badly.
- give constructive feedback, not just "this is bad".
- keep it on topic and friendly.

## enforcement

if someone breaks these rules, the maintainers can warn them, remove their comments or prs, or ban them if it's bad enough. we'll try to be fair and proportional, and we won't do it as a power trip.

## reporting

see something that crosses a line? tell us. you can reach us at [hello@devmatei.com](mailto:hello@devmatei.com). reports stay private and we'll take them seriously. if you just have questions, reach out too, we're happy to talk it out.

## scope

this applies to the repo, issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere people talk about this project.
